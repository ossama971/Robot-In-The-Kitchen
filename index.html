Robot In The Kitchen

Entry page for the five exercise pages in the robot assignment. Each card opens a standalone static page for the corresponding stage of the scene, from the base room setup through XR tele-operation.

Exercise 1

Kitchen Scene

PerspectiveCamera (FOV 50°), OrbitControls, PCFSoftShadowMap renderer, tiled PlaneGeometry floor, BoxGeometry walls and counter, CylinderGeometry tableware — all using MeshStandardMaterial.

Exercise 2

Robot Hierarchy

Articulated scene graph: nested THREE.Group pivot chains for shoulder → elbow → wrist. Chrome joint spheres, emissive eye material, and AxesHelpers at each joint origin.

Exercise 3

PBR Lighting & Shadows

SpotLight (1024² shadow map, bias −0.00015) over the counter. Runtime scene traversal classifies meshes by bounding-box dimensions. lil-gui exposes light position, shadow quality, and per-group roughness/metalness.

Open page
Exercise 4

Animation & Interaction

Time-based sinusoidal arm sweep in auto mode; snapshot-on-transition to manual mode with per-joint GUI sliders. Anatomical rotation limits enforced on shoulder abduction/flexion and elbow flexion axes.

Open page
Exercise 5

WebXR Tele-operation

VR and AR entry via WebXR Device API. Two-segment analytical IK (law of cosines) maps controller world positions to shoulder/elbow angles with LERP smoothing. AR passthrough hides virtual walls and calibrates eye height on the first XR frame.
